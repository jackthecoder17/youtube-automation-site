Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

youtube-automation is a personal tool run by its owner on their own computer. It signs in to the owner's own Google account and uses one permission, youtube.upload, solely to upload videos the owner created to the owner's own YouTube channel.

What data is used

  • An OAuth access token for the owner's Google account, stored only on the owner's computer, used only to upload videos.
  • No other Google user data is read, collected, stored, or shared.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. The app does not use Google user data for advertising or for training AI models.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
